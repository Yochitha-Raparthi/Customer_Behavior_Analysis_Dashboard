# Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis

## 📊 Project Overview

**Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis** is a data analytics project focused on understanding customer purchasing patterns and generating actionable business insights.

The project analyzes customer transaction data to identify trends in **customer demographics, product categories, purchasing behavior, subscription status, discounts, shipping preferences, customer loyalty, and revenue contribution**.

The primary objective is to help businesses improve **sales, customer engagement, marketing strategies, and long-term customer loyalty** through data-driven decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

A leading retail company wants to better understand its customers' shopping behavior to improve sales, customer satisfaction, and long-term loyalty.

The business is interested in understanding how factors such as **discounts, reviews, seasons, customer demographics, product categories, and purchasing behavior** influence consumer decisions and repeat purchases.

The main business question is:

> **How can the company leverage consumer shopping data to identify trends, improve customer engagement, and optimize marketing and product strategies?**

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Analyze customer purchasing behavior and spending patterns
* Identify high-performing product categories
* Understand subscription and non-subscription customer behavior
* Analyze revenue across different age groups and genders
* Identify customer segments based on purchase history
* Analyze the impact of discounts on purchasing behavior
* Compare shipping types and purchase amounts
* Identify top-rated and frequently purchased products
* Build an interactive Power BI dashboard
* Generate actionable business recommendations

---

## 🗂️ Dataset

The dataset contains **3,900 customer purchase records** with **18 columns**.

### Key Features

* Customer demographics

  * Age
  * Gender
  * Location
  * Subscription Status

* Purchase details

  * Item Purchased
  * Category
  * Purchase Amount
  * Season
  * Size
  * Color

* Shopping behavior

  * Discount Applied
  * Previous Purchases
  * Frequency of Purchases
  * Review Rating
  * Shipping Type

The dataset initially contained **37 missing values in the Review Rating column**.

## 🐍 1. Data Preparation Using Python

The raw dataset was cleaned and prepared using **Python and Pandas**.

### Key Steps

* Loaded and explored the dataset
* Checked data types and dataset structure
* Identified missing values
* Handled missing review ratings using the median rating of each product category
* Standardized column names using snake_case
* Created an `age_group` feature by grouping customer ages
* Created a `purchase_frequency_days` feature
* Checked for redundant columns
* Removed `promo_code_used` after identifying redundancy with `discount_applied`
* Loaded the cleaned dataset into PostgreSQL

---

## 🗄️ 2. Data Analysis Using SQL

The cleaned data was stored in **PostgreSQL** for structured business analysis.

SQL queries were used to answer important business questions, including:

### Business Questions

1. What is the revenue generated by male and female customers?
2. Which customers use discounts but still spend above the average purchase amount?
3. What are the top 5 products based on average review rating?
4. How does average purchase amount differ between Standard and Express shipping?
5. How do subscribers compare with non-subscribers in terms of spending and revenue?
6. Which products have the highest percentage of discounted purchases?
7. How can customers be segmented into New, Returning, and Loyal customers?
8. What are the top 3 products in each category?
9. Are customers with more than 5 purchases more likely to subscribe?
10. Which age group contributes the highest revenue?

## 📌 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following strategies can be considered:

### 1. Boost Subscriptions

Promote exclusive benefits and offers to encourage more customers to subscribe.

### 2. Strengthen Customer Loyalty

Introduce loyalty programs and rewards for repeat customers to move them toward the **Loyal** customer segment.

### 3. Review Discount Strategy

Balance discount-based sales growth with profitability and margin control.

### 4. Improve Product Positioning

Promote highly rated and best-selling products through targeted marketing campaigns.

### 5. Target High-Value Customer Groups

Focus marketing campaigns on age groups and customer segments that contribute higher revenue.
